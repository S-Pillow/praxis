# Accepted research decisions — 2026-11-18

**Purpose:** preserve user's explicit choices; these notes do not amend AIPF v8.10.1.

1. Build an **original normalized unified design catalog** collecting useful distinct styles/templates/palettes/font pairings/knowledge from multiple repositories.
2. Do not arbitrarily cap the catalog; preserve unique options and also preserve each upstream entry where overlapping designs exist.
3. **Build first:** specialists primarily teach the AI *how to produce better work during implementation*. They should also **critique and audit** the result before finish.
4. **Optional anti-generic diagnostics.** Aesthetic anti-pattern signals are opt-in/contextual, not blanket prohibitions.
5. **Stronger brand-truth extraction.** Check authorized source brand tokens/assets rather than guessing. Existing approved system wins over catalog defaults. Any possible AIPF rule revision is a later, separate discussion.
6. **Critique vs audit separation.** Design judgment and measurable technical tests are different procedures.
7. **Do not add mandatory degraded-review disclosure** as a specialist requirement. Never misrepresent review independence, tool availability, or evidence.
8. AIPF remains governing v8.10.1; specialists are additive, not competing governance or new decision authority.
9. Unified catalog research remains distinct from specialist-instruction evaluation.
10. Before redistributing, review exact asset and example licenses, per-entry provenance, upstream attribution and license notices. Unreviewed photos/fonts/icons/screenshots/demo bundles are not packaged.
11. No unapproved plugin install, deployment, public release, or framework edit.
