# Unified Design Catalog — separate research workstream

The catalog is an independent knowledge-and-assets research track, not the specialist operating instructions.

## Why

A source may provide **content** (themes, palettes, font pairings, design systems, previews) and/or **methods** (how to design, critique, audit and verify). Keep them separate to avoid copying governance and to enable narrow retrieval.

## Include

- themes, templates, UI styles, palettes, typography, illustration, components, chart types, motion patterns, design system tokens, examples, craft knowledge
- original name, direct source pointer, descriptions, actual token data when verified, preview reference, compatibility restrictions, license and attribution
- duplicate/equivalent links without silently discarding upstream variants

## Release gate

A metadata-only or reference-only record is **not** cleared for binary/code/content redistribution. No photos, font files, icon packages, screenshots, branded design-system assets, external images, or runnable demos should be copied before exact rights review.
