Define each unit around the largest meaningful outcome that can safely share one authorization, risk, execution, durability, evidence, rollback/recovery, and stop boundary; do not split merely by component, file, implementation step, intermediate derivation, or verification step. Use low-risk batching only under Section 9; keep item-level acceptance and evidence separate. |

Version note: v8.10.1 is a targeted Chapter 14 consistency correction. It broadens the stable-UI-checkpoint rule so an explicitly authorized redesign or evidence-backed improvement may proceed when tied to any applicable Chapter 14 acceptance criterion; adds Visual direction / substantial redesign as the canonical classification for material visual-system or design-language changes that may preserve product behavior/workflow; and clarifies that exact values used for system extraction are stronger evidence than visual guessing only when authorized and usable for the project. No other normative control model is changed. See Appendix A.
